      MATCH RULES

      • +3 points per elimination
      • +1 point per energy crystal
      • 100 integrity, 20 damage per plasma hit
      • Respawn after 3s with 2s spawn protection
      • Highest score when the timer hits zero wins
      • Ties are broken by kills, then fewer deaths
      • Matches can end early at the configured score limit
